| Cover | What it generally covers | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Comprehensive** | Damage to your car, damage to other vehicles/property, theft, fire, and some weather damage (depending on policy) | Newer or higher-value used cars |
| **Third Party Property** | Damage you cause to someone else’s car/property | Older, low-value cars where you can afford to replace your own car |
| **Third Party Fire & Theft** | Third party damage plus your car if stolen or damaged by fire | Older cars with some remaining value |
| **CTP (Compulsory Third Party)** | Injury compensation for people hurt in an accident | Mandatory with registration; does not cover vehicle damage |
