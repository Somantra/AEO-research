| Feature | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Agreed vs market value** | Agreed value gives you a set insured amount; market value pays what the insurer considers the car was worth immediately before the loss. |
| **Excess** | A higher excess generally lowers the premium, but means more out-of-pocket after a claim. |
| **Choice of repairer** | Useful if you want your own trusted repair shop rather than an insurer-selected repairer. |
| **New-car replacement** | Particularly valuable for a newer vehicle if it's written off. |
| **Windscreen/glass excess** | Some policies let you pay no excess for eligible glass claims. |
| **Hire car** | Check whether you're covered while your car is being repaired—and for how long. |
| **Roadside assistance** | Often optional rather than automatically included. |
| **Personal belongings** | Check the dollar limit and exclusions. |
| **Driver restrictions** | Limiting who can drive the car can reduce premiums, but breaches can affect claims. |
| **Modifications/accessories** | Make sure aftermarket equipment is declared and covered. |
