| Feature | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Agreed value** | Gives you a defined payout if the bike is written off, rather than relying on the insurer's assessment of market value. |
| **Theft & attempted theft** | Particularly important for motorcycles. |
| **Accidental damage** | Covers crashes, including many at-fault incidents, subject to the PDS. |
| **High third-party liability** | $20–30m is common among major policies. |
| **Helmet/riding gear** | Useful because quality helmets, jackets, boots and pants can be expensive. |
| **Accessories & modifications** | Make sure exhausts, panniers, crash bars, luggage, electronics etc. are declared and covered. |
| **New-bike replacement** | Valuable if you're buying a relatively new motorcycle. |
| **Emergency accommodation/towing** | Worth having if you tour or ride long distances. |
| **Choice of repairer** | Important if you want a particular motorcycle repair shop. |
| **Reasonable excess** | Compare the *total* excess, not just the advertised premium. |
| **Rider restrictions** | Check learner/provisional rider excesses and who is permitted to ride the bike. |
| **Usage restrictions** | Check commuting, business use, touring, track days and other recreational use. |
