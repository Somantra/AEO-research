| **High excess** | Look at basic excess, age excess, inexperienced driver excess, and special excesses |
| **Market value payout** | A cheaper policy may pay less after a total loss than an agreed-value policy |

| **Optional extras removed** | Windscreen cover, hire car after an accident, roadside assistance may cost extra |
