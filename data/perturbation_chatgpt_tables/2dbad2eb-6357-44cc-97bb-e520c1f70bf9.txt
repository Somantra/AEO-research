| Cover | What it generally protects against |
|---|---|
| **Building insurance** | Damage to the structure from insured events such as fire, storms, floods (if included), and other listed events |
| **Landlord contents** | Items you own at the property (for example carpets, curtains, appliances or furnishings supplied to tenants) |
| **Loss of rent** | Rental income lost when the property becomes uninhabitable due to an insured event |
| **Tenant damage / malicious damage** | Damage caused by tenants, depending on policy terms |
| **Rent default** | Some policies offer optional cover if tenants stop paying rent or leave unexpectedly |
| **Legal liability** | Claims if someone is injured or suffers damage connected to your property |
